Yatra Registration

Online registration is mandatory for all pilgrims visiting Char Dham temples

Why Register?

  • Mandatory for temple entry and darshan
  • Instant e-pass generation
  • Priority darshan options available
  • Health & safety monitoring
  • Emergency contact tracking
  • SMS alerts for weather & temple updates

Documents Required

  • Valid Photo ID (Aadhaar/PAN/Passport)
  • Recent passport-size photograph
  • Mobile number for OTP verification
  • Medical fitness certificate (for senior citizens)

Registration Fee

₹50 per person

Valid for entire yatra season
