Sacred Legends

Ancient stories and mythological significance of the holy shrines

🕉️

श्री बद्रीनाथ

According to Hindu mythology, Badrinath is the abode of Lord Vishnu. Once, Goddess Lakshmi protected Lord Vishnu from the harsh weather by transforming into a Badri tree (Indian Jujube). Hence, Lord Vishnu came to be known as Badrinath - the Lord of Badri forest.

Adi Shankaracharya rediscovered this sacred shrine in the 8th century and established it as one of the Char Dhams. The temple is believed to be one of the 108 Divya Desams dedicated to Lord Vishnu.

🔱

श्री केदारनाथ

After the Mahabharata war, the Pandavas sought Lord Shiva's blessings for absolution. Lord Shiva, avoiding them, disguised himself as a bull. When Bhima tried to catch him, Lord Shiva dived into the ground leaving behind his hump, which is worshipped at Kedarnath.

This makes Kedarnath one of the 12 Jyotirlingas and the first among the Panch Kedar temples. The temple was also established by Adi Shankaracharya and has withstood numerous natural calamities.
