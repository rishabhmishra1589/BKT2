## 3. Temple Details - Shri Kedarnath Dham

### Location
- **District:** Rudraprayag, Uttarakhand
- **Region:** Garhwal Himalayas
- **Altitude:** 3,583 meters (11,755 feet) above sea level

### Religious Significance
- **Deity:** Lord Shiva
- **Status:** 11th of the 12 Jyotirlingas
- **Part of:** Panch Kedar (Five Kedars)
- **Established by:** Pandavas and re-established by Adi Shankaracharya (8th century)

### Access
- **Trek Distance:** 16 km from Gaurikund
- **Helicopter Service:** Available from Phata/Sersi/Guptkashi
- **Duration:** 7-10 minutes by helicopter

### Season
- **Open Season:** May to November (weather dependent)
- **Closure:** During winter due to heavy snowfall

---

## 4. Temple Details - Shri Badarinath Dham

### Location
- **District:** Chamoli, Uttarakhand
- **Near:** Indo-China border
- **Altitude:** 3,300 meters (10,827 feet) above sea level

### Religious Significance
- **Deity:** Lord Vishnu (Badrinarayan)
- **Status:** One of 108 Divya Desams
- **Part of:** Char Dham pilgrimage circuit
- **Established by:** Adi Shankaracharya (8th century)

### Season
- **Open Season:** April/May to November (weather dependent)
- **Temple Timings:** 4:30 AM to 9:00 PM (seasonal variations apply)

## 5. Helicopter Booking Service

### Official Booking Platform
- **Website:** https://heliyatra.irctc.co.in
- **Operated by:** IRCTC (Indian Railway Catering and Tourism Corporation)

### Guidelines
- Advance booking required
- Subject to weather conditions
- Baggage limit: 5 kg per person
- Valid Photo ID mandatory
- Children below 2 years not permitted
- Refund policy applicable for weather cancellations

### Routes
- Phata to Kedarnath
- Sersi to Kedarnath
- Guptkashi to Kedarnath

## 11. Complete List of 45+ Temples Managed by BKTC

### Panch Kedar (5 Sacred Kedars)
1. **Shri Kedarnath Temple** - Main shrine (3,583 m)
2. **Shri Tunganath Temple** - Highest Shiva temple (3,680 m)
3. **Shri Madmaheshwar Temple** - Navel form of Shiva
4. **Shri Rudranath Temple** - Face form of Shiva
5. **Shri Kalpeshwar Temple** - Hair locks form (open year-round)

### Panch Badari (5 Sacred Badaris)
6. **Shri Badarinath Temple** - Main shrine (3,300 m)
7. **Bhavishya Badari Temple** - Future Badari at Subhain (2,744 m)
8. **Yogbadari Temple** - At Pandukeshwar (1,929 m)
9. **Dhyan Badari Temple** - At Urgam Village
10. **Vridha Badari Temple** - At Animath
11. **Adi Badari Temple** - At Karnaprayag

### Sacred Sites at Badrinath
12. **Tapt Kund** - Hot spring tank
13. **Brahma Kapal** - Sacred ghat on Alaknanda
14. **Mata Murti Temple** - Mother of Badrinarayan (opposite Mana)
15. **Shri Aadi Kedareshwar Temple** - At Badrinath
16. **Panch Shilas** - Five sacred stones
17. **Panch Dharas** - Five sacred water streams

### Other Important Temples
18. **Narsingh Temple** - At Joshimath (winter seat of Badrinath)
19. **Shri Vishwanath Ji Temple** - At Guptkashi
20. **Gauri Mayi Temple** - At Gaurikund
21. **Triyuginarayan Temple** - Marriage place of Shiva-Parvati (eternal flame)
22. **Onkareshwar Temple** - At Ukhimath (winter seat of Kedarnath)
23. **Kalishila Temple** - At Kalishila (4 km from Kalimath)
24. **Mahadev Temple** - At Jyoteshwar
25. **Basudhara Falls Site** - Dharamshila at waterfall bottom

### Minor Temples & Sites
- Minor temples within the precincts of Shri Kedarnath Temple
- Minor temples within the precincts of Shri Vishwanath Ji at Guptkashi
- The Temple of Shri Tunganath at Makku (winter puja location)
- Various other sacred shrines across Garhwal region

**Total:** 47+ temples including main shrines
