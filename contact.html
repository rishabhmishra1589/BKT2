Frequently Asked Questions

When do the temples open?

Badrinath and Kedarnath temples typically open in April-May and close in October-November. Exact dates depend on auspicious timings and weather conditions. Check our website for annual opening/closing dates.

Is online registration mandatory?

Yes, online yatra registration is mandatory for all pilgrims. You need to register at least 3 days before your planned visit. The e-pass will be verified at entry points.

What is the best time to visit?

May-June and September-October are ideal months. May-June sees peak pilgrim season. September-October offers pleasant weather with fewer crowds. Avoid monsoon season (July-August) due to landslide risks.

Are there accommodation facilities?

Yes, we provide dormitories, regular rooms, deluxe rooms, and VIP suites at base camps and temple locations. Advance booking is recommended during peak season.

Can senior citizens and children visit?

Yes, but medical fitness certificate is recommended for senior citizens. Helicopter services and palki/pony services are available for those unable to trek. Children below 2 years are not permitted on helicopter services.
