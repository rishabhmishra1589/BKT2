## 🎯 **Matra (Vowel Sign) Rules Applied:**

### **Correct Matra Usage:**

1. **Anusvara (ं) vs Chandrabindu (ँ):**
   - ं used before consonants (संपर्क)
   - ँ used for nasal vowels (ऊँचाई)

2. **Short vs Long Vowels:**
   - इ (short i) in compound words
   - ई (long ī) at end of words/verses

3. **Visarga (ः):**
   - Properly placed after vowels (नमः)
   - Represents aspiration sound

4. **Ya (य) vs I-matra (ि):**
   - Correctly distinguished throughout

## 🎓 **Key Corrections Explained:**

### **1. गंगा vs गङ्गा:**
In Sanskrit, when 'ं' (anusvara) comes before a velar consonant (क, ख, ग, घ, ङ), it should be written as 'ङ्' (velar nasal) for proper pronunciation.

**Example:**
- गंगा (simplified, acceptable) 
- गङ्गा (classical, more accurate) ✅

### **2. Chandrabindu (ँ) Usage:**
Chandrabindu represents nasalization of the vowel itself, while anusvara represents a following nasal consonant.

**Example:**
- हंसी (hassi - laughter with nasal consonant)
- हँसी (hãsi - smiling with nasal vowel) ✅

### **3. विजयन्तेतराम् Issue:**
"तराम्" doesn't exist in Sanskrit as a meaningful word in this context. The correct ending is simply "विजयन्ते" (may they be victorious).
