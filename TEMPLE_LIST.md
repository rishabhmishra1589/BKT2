## 🔱 PANCH KEDAR (Five Sacred Kedars - Lord Shiva)

### 1. Shri Kedarnath Temple ⭐ Main Shrine
- **Location:** Rudraprayag District, Garhwal Himalayas
- **Altitude:** 3,583 meters (11,755 feet)
- **Deity:** Lord Shiva (11th of 12 Jyotirlingas)
- **Significance:** One of Panch Kedar, hump form of Shiva
- **Trek:** 16 km from Gaurikund
- **Contact:** +91-8534001008
- **Open Season:** May to November

### 2. Shri Tunganath Temple
- **Location:** Tunganath, Rudraprayag (Chandranath Parbat)
- **Altitude:** 3,680 meters (Highest Shiva temple in the world)
- **Deity:** Lord Shiva (Arms form)
- **Trek:** 3 km from Chopta
- **Distance from Rishikesh:** 212 km via Chamoli-Gopeshwar-Chopta
- **Contact:** +91-7302257116 (8 AM to 6 PM)
- **2019 Season:** May 10 - Oct 29
- **Winter Puja Location:** Makkumath (45 km from Ukhimath)

### 3. Shri Madmaheshwar Temple
- **Location:** Madmaheshwar
- **Deity:** Lord Shiva (Navel/Belly Button form)
- **Significance:** Part of Panch Kedar
- **Contact:** +91-7302257116

### 4. Shri Rudranath Temple
- **Location:** Rudranath
- **Deity:** Lord Shiva (Face form)
- **Significance:** Part of Panch Kedar
- **Contact:** +91-7302257116

### 5. Shri Kalpeshwar Temple
- **Location:** Kalpeshwar, Urgam Valley
- **Deity:** Lord Shiva (Hair/Locks form)
- **Significance:** Part of Panch Kedar, Open year-round (only Kedar open in winter)
- **Contact:** +91-7302257116

## 🕉️ PANCH BADARI (Five Sacred Badaris - Lord Vishnu)

### 6. Shri Badarinath Temple ⭐ Main Shrine
- **Location:** Chamoli District, near Indo-China border
- **Altitude:** 3,300 meters (10,827 feet)
- **Deity:** Lord Vishnu (Badrinarayan) - One of 108 Divya Desams
- **Significance:** Part of Char Dham, established by Adi Shankaracharya
- **Contact:** +91-8979001008
- **Temple Timings:** 4:30 AM - 9:00 PM
- **Open Season:** April/May to November

### 7. Bhavishya Badari Temple (Future Badari)
- **Location:** Subhain near Tapovan, 17 km east of Joshimath
- **Altitude:** 2,744 meters
- **Deity:** Narsiarsimh (Lion-headed form)
- **Significance:** "Future Badari" - believed to be worshipped when Badrinath becomes inaccessible
- **Features:** Self-emerging idol, surrounded by dense forests, sulphurous hot springs at Tapovan
- **Best Time:** March to May, September to November
- **Contact:** +91-7302257116

### 8. Yogbadari Temple (Yog Badari)
- **Location:** Pandukeshwar (24 km from Badrinath)
- **Altitude:** 1,929 meters
- **Deity:** Lord Vishnu in meditative posture
- **Significance:** Named after Pandu; place where Pandavas retired
- **Best Time:** May, June, September, October
- **Contact:** +91-7302257116

### 9. Dhyan Badari Temple
- **Location:** Urgam Village
- **Deity:** Lord Vishnu (in meditation)
- **Significance:** Part of Panch Badari
- **Contact:** +91-7302257116

### 10. Vridha Badari Temple (Old Badari)
- **Location:** Animath
- **Deity:** Old form of Lord Badrinath
- **Significance:** Part of Panch Badari
- **Contact:** +91-7302257116

### 11. Adi Badari Temple (Ancient Badari)
- **Location:** Karnaprayag
- **Deity:** Ancient form of Lord Badrinath
- **Significance:** Part of Panch Badari
- **Contact:** +91-7302257116

## 🙏 SACRED SITES AT BADRINATH

### 12. Tapt Kund (Hot Spring)
- **Location:** At Badrinath temple complex
- **Significance:** Natural thermal hot spring with medicinal properties
- **Ritual:** Pilgrims take ritual bath before Badrinath darshan
- **Features:** Sacred tank and spring

### 13. Brahma Kapal
- **Location:** Banks of Alaknanda River, near Badrinath temple
- **Significance:** Flat platform for rituals and shraddha for departed souls
- **Activities:** Hindus perform rituals and pay homage to ancestors
- **Best Time:** May, June, September, October

### 14. Mata Murti Temple
- **Location:** Right bank of Alaknanda, opposite Mana village
- **Deity:** Mother of Shri Badarinathji
- **Festival:** Vaman Dwadashi (annual visit by Badarinathji)
- **Significance:** Powers of granting sincere wishes or Vairagya
- **Priest:** Rawal (head priest) of Badarinathji performs puja
- **Best Time:** May, June, September, October

### 15. Shri Aadi Kedareshwar Temple
- **Location:** At Badrinath
- **Deity:** Ancient form of Kedar
- **Managed by:** BKTC
- **Contact:** +91-7302257116

### 16. Panch Shilas (Five Sacred Stones)
- **Location:** Within Badrinath Puri
- **Significance:** Five sacred stones with religious importance in Hindu mythology

### 17. Panch Dharas (Five Sacred Water Streams)
- **Location:** Within Badrinath Puri
- **Significance:** Five sacred water streams, each with unique spiritual importance

## 📖 RELIGIOUS SIGNIFICANCE

### Panch Kedar Legend:
After the Mahabharata war, the Pandavas sought Lord Shiva's blessings. To avoid them, Lord Shiva took the form of a bull and disappeared into the earth at different locations. The five temples mark where his body parts appeared:
1. **Kedarnath** - Hump
2. **Tunganath** - Arms
3. **Rudranath** - Face
4. **Madmaheshwar** - Navel
5. **Kalpeshwar** - Hair

### Panch Badari Legend:
Five sacred forms of Lord Vishnu worshipped in the Garhwal Himalayas, each representing different aspects and stages of devotion.
