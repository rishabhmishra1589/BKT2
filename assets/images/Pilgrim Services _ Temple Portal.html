Frequently Asked Questions

How to book online darshan?

Visit our website, select the temple and date, choose your time slot, fill in the details, and make payment. You'll receive a confirmation via email and SMS.

Can I cancel or reschedule my booking?

Yes, you can cancel or reschedule up to 48 hours before the scheduled time. Cancellation charges may apply as per policy.

Are accommodation facilities available for families?

Yes, we have family rooms and suites available. You can book online or contact our helpdesk for assistance.

What payment methods are accepted?

We accept credit/debit cards, net banking, UPI, and digital wallets. All transactions are secure and encrypted.

Is helicopter booking available throughout the year?

Helicopter services are available during the pilgrimage season (April to November), subject to weather conditions.

What documents are required for booking?

Valid government-issued photo ID (Aadhaar, PAN, Passport, or Driving License) is mandatory for all bookings and temple entry.
