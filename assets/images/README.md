## 🎨 **IMAGE SPECIFICATIONS**

### **Technical Requirements:**
- **Format:** JPG for photos, PNG for transparent elements
- **Quality:** High resolution (minimum 1200px width)
- **Optimization:** Compress images to 200-500KB for web performance
- **Aspect Ratios:**
  - Hero images: 16:9 (1920x1080)
  - Service cards: 4:3 or 16:9
  - Gallery: Square or 4:3

### **Content Guidelines:**
- ✅ Real temple photographs
- ✅ Authentic devotional scenes
- ✅ High-quality, well-lit images
- ✅ Respectful depiction of religious practices
- ❌ No copyright violations
- ❌ No offensive or graphic content
- ❌ No low-quality or pixelated images

## 🔧 **HOW TO ADD IMAGES**

### **Step 1: Download Images**
Download high-quality images matching the descriptions above.

### **Step 2: Optimize Images**
Use tools to compress images:
- **TinyPNG** (tinypng.com) - Online compression
- **ImageOptim** (Mac) or **FileOptimizer** (Windows)
- Target size: 200-500KB per image

### **Step 3: Rename & Place Files**
Rename downloaded files to match the exact filenames listed above and place them in the `assets/images/` folder:

```
assets/images/
├── badrinath-01.jpg
├── badrinath-02.jpg
├── kedarnath-01.jpg
├── kedarnath-02.jpg
├── yatri-01.jpg
├── arti-01.jpg
├── pooja-01.jpg
├── sadhu-01.jpg
├── aghori-01.jpg
├── himalaya-01.jpg
├── himalaya-02.jpg
├── decor-mandala.png
├── decor-bells.png
└── favicon.png
```

### **Step 4: Test the Website**
Open `index.html` in a browser and verify all images load correctly.
